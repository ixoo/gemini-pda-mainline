Source facts
------------

- Clock-backend driver registration precedes BigiDVFS-backend registration,
  which precedes observer registration in device-initcall order.
- The `probe-enter` record is the first operation in the observer probe.
- Before any protected read, the clock-backend probe allocates state, maps the
  MCUMIXED and CSPM resources, acquires the I2C clock handle, initializes locks,
  and publishes driver data.
- The BigiDVFS probe validates `method = "smc"`, allocates state, initializes a
  mutex, and publishes driver data. It makes no secure call during probe.
- The protected clock and BigiDVFS reads occur only later from the observer.
- The image links all three probes and all three initcalls; compile/link
  omission is excluded.

Inference
---------

The runtime `neither` result places the first unobserved boundary no later
than the observer's first operation. It does not by itself distinguish an
earlier boot failure, clock/BigiDVFS registration or probe behavior, observer
binding failure, or refusal by the observer record's own minimal gate.

Historical post-LK live-FDT evidence establishes the exact Gemini compatible,
ramoops reservation, and no-map properties for closely related serviceable
candidates, and deployment proved all four slot headers empty. Those facts
make the minimal gate plausible but do not prove its evaluation in this exact
failed cycle.

Decision-bearing successor
--------------------------

Use an isolated, default-off clock-backend-entry profile with no observer,
no BigiDVFS node, and zero protected calls. Retain at most two fixed writes:

1. one immediately before clock-backend platform-driver registration;
2. one as the clock-backend probe's first operation.

Neither record moves the unknown boundary before the clock initcall or into
the shared safety gate. The first record alone locates failure in registration,
matching, or entry before the probe operation. Both records prove clock probe
entry and move the next split inside that read-free probe. A serviceable boot
with both records closes the clock-backend probe prerequisite before BigiDVFS
is isolated separately. Do not re-enable the observer or either protected read
in this successor.
