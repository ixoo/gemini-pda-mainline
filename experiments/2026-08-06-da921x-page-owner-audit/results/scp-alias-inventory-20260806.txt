## Limits

The literal pools and control-flow windows explain the observed local aliases,
but a raw CM4 image does not provide complete symbols or secure-world address
translation. This inventory therefore narrows, rather than eliminates, the
computed/secure-alias residual. It does not authorize callback registration,
a DA921x write, CPU8/CPU9 admission, or a device boot.
