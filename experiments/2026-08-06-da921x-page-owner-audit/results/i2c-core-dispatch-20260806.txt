The pinned core source proves the dispatch chain used by the provider's
`__i2c_transfer()` call:

function=__i2c_transfer
adapter_dispatch=adap->algo->master_xfer(adap, msgs, num)
public_wrapper=ret = __i2c_transfer(adap, msgs, num)

This closes the question of a provider-side `__i2c_transfer()` bypassing the
MT65xx adapter's `mt6797_dvfsp_handoff_require_ready()` check. It does not
prove a separate per-transfer lease, DA921x page ownership, writable transport,
post-settle readback, or rollback.
