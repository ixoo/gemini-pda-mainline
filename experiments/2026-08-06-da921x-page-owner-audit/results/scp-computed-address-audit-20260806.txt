## Limits

This is a bounded raw-image analysis, not a complete firmware decompilation or
secure-world address-map proof. The newly followed literals and immediate
construction do not expose the AP I2C6/CSPM/PCM/CSRAM targets, but absence from
these paths does not exclude a computed or secure alias elsewhere. The result
therefore strengthens the negative discriminator without authorizing callback
registration, a DA921x write, CPU8/CPU9 admission, or a device boot.
