## Limits

The bounded disassembly confirms the existing ATF interpretation more
precisely: its direct CSPM accesses are a keyed `+0` control write and a
secure-semaphore `+0x448` write/poll sequence. It does not expose the vendor
Linux `SEMA_I2C_DRV` pause-source words or a direct PCM kick/reset. This does
not exclude computed or secure aliases elsewhere, and it does not identify the
missing receiver owner in SCP or another firmware domain.
