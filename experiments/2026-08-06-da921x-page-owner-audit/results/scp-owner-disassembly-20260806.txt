## Limits

This is a bounded raw-image analysis, not a complete firmware decompilation or
symbol recovery. It distinguishes the tested aliases from the DMA, clock, and
NVIC paths using nearby call sites and log strings, but it does not prove that
every computed or secure alias is absent. A separately reviewed firmware
protocol or an attributable receiver implementation remains required before
any DA921x write or provider-owner registration.
