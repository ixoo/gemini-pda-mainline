Interpretation
--------------

The STOP between Kepler's separate pointer-write and read ioctls is not an
unsupported DA9214 access shape. It cannot explain the stable CPU-visible
0x05 result merely by assuming that the chip requires a repeated START.

This document result does not establish the physical source of Kepler's
0x05 byte. The byte equals the immediately preceding register pointer, while
the known Gemian page-2 value at offset 0x05 is 0xd9. A true device response
and controller, APDMA, cache, or buffer residue remain to be distinguished
with independent hardware evidence.
