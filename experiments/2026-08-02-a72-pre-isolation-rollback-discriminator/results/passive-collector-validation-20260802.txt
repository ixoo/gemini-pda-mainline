The remote program checks exact kernel, root, CPU, and proc identity, then
copies the observer immediately. Optional USB and battery reporting occurs
only after two copies exist. A second copy after two seconds must be
byte-identical for every terminal state. The host collector pins the remote
program, validator, bounded executor, ignored capture location, private key
mode, and known-hosts mode before making a bounded connection.

Semantic validation
-------------------
- ABI-v3 state, count, overflow and transaction metadata are parsed exactly;
- every record must be contiguous, CPU8-only, one-transaction, and observe
  CPU8/9 offline;
- every phase at or beyond external-isolation clear is forbidden;
- rolled-back requires the exact 30-record owner ordering, entry state,
  attempt-owned forward transitions and inverses, final equality, three-way
  clock equality, five-component fixed snapshot, and disposition 1;
- rejected-prestate requires the exact 14-record no-write ordering and
  disposition 3;
- fault-retain requires the complete entry gate, bounded record count, final
  immutable disposition 2, and reset recovery rather than acceptance;
- wait-up/capture-up are preserved as incomplete without stimulus; and
- legacy frozen states are classified as boundary violations, never success.
