Runtime hypothesis and unique evidence
--------------------------------------
The early unlatched CPU8 request should return -EAGAIN without observer or owner
records and without consuming the one-shot. The later natural HPS CPU8-up
transaction should then be the only attributable attempt. CPU8 and CPU9 must
remain offline; this candidate intentionally stops before external isolation
and never invokes PSCI CPU_ON.

The unique evidence is an immutable ABI-v3 terminal result containing owner
and rollback records from that later HPS transaction. An attributable
-EALREADY or -EAGAIN HPS end, a two-record frozen-up-failed result, missing
owner records, CPU8/9 online, a forbidden-boundary event, or unstable snapshots
rejects the experiment. Ordinary Gemian visuals and delayed USB/console are
serviceability observations, not kernel identity.

Result matrix
-------------
rolled-back:
  Close only the pre-isolation BUCKB/reset rollback claim and design the next
  separately bounded isolation-stage experiment. Do not claim CPU8 online.
rejected-prestate:
  Revise the exact pre-state assumption; do not retry unchanged.
fault-retain:
  Recover through native reset, retain evidence, and do not retry unchanged.
EALREADY-or-EAGAIN-attributable-HPS-end:
  Reject the latch/orchestration correction; do not retry unchanged.
forbidden-boundary-or-CPU8/9-online:
  Reject as a boundary violation and recover to known-good Gemian.
missing-or-unstable-evidence:
  Inconclusive; recover and redesign before another boot.
