A72 pre-isolation rollback compiler, owner-lock, and timing review
Recorded: 2026-08-02 America/New_York

Scope
-----
This review covers the exact rollback-enabled source built from repository
commit 090f148d9ce8473331a7c9efdf15aa7acb3c3806. It does not authorize a boot
image, device deployment, or runtime action.

Compiler and stack review
-------------------------
- Both rollback and exact parent-observer trees compile and link.
- The sole extracted compiler diagnostic is byte-identical in both trees: the
  inherited vendor summary of 69 section mismatches.
- Both archives contain 2484 source-compared stack-use reports.
- cpu_psci_cpu_boot grows from 96 to 128 bytes.
- New owner helpers use at most 112 bytes.
- The unchanged whole-kernel maximum remains ANCService_read at 1488 bytes.

Owner-lock review
-----------------
- DA921x compare/update and snapshot operations use da9214_i2c_access.
- SPM compare/update and snapshot operations use __spm_lock.
- TOPRGU compare/update uses rgu_reg_operation_spinlock.
- DCM inspection uses mt6797_a72_obs_mp2_dcm_lock.
- Clock capture uses an immediate trylock on g_mt6797_0x1001AXXX_lock and an
  immediate hardware-semaphore attempt; contention fails the experiment.
- reset_flags is compared, claimed, and restored under reset_lock.
- Each owner releases its lock before appending observer evidence. The
  orchestrator invokes owners sequentially, so it adds no cross-owner or
  observer-under-owner lock nesting.

Successful-path operation bound
-------------------------------
- DA921x: 33 reads and 2 writes. Five exact compare/readback operations use six
  reads each; the final fixed snapshot adds three reads. The two writes are
  BUCKB enable and the attempt-owned inverse disable.
- SPM: 20 reads and 2 writes. Compare/readback covers only offsets 0x218 and
  0x290; the final fixed snapshot adds the established six-register snapshot.
- TOPRGU: 8 reads and 2 writes around the exact PWRAP-reset bit.
- Secure state: 39 calls, comprising twelve fixed registers plus one sentinel
  reread at entry, final comparison, and final fixed snapshot.
- Clock: three immediate probes at entry, final comparison, and final fixed
  snapshot; each has only the established 200 ns post-acquisition delay.
- DCM: three owner-locked reads at entry, final comparison, and final snapshot.

Timing and forbidden-boundary review
------------------------------------
- The stimulus is one-shot for CPU8. CPU9 and repeated CPU8 requests fail
  before the experiment path.
- The only deliberate millisecond delay is the existing 1 ms BUCKB settle.
  There is no polling loop and no semaphore wait loop.
- No broad snapshot occurs inside either 240 microsecond SRAM-LDO interval;
  this discriminator never reaches SRAM-LDO at all.
- The path never clears external isolation, invokes PSCI CPU_ON, enables MP2
  DCM, performs an iDVFS action, or onlines CPU8 or CPU9.
- Pre-state rejection performs no regulator, SPM, or TOPRGU state change.
- A changed value is inverted only when its attempt-owned flag is set and the
  current readback still equals the state written by this attempt.
- The final exact comparisons and fixed snapshot precede one immutable
  terminal disposition. There is no retry.

Decision
--------

stack_review=passed
