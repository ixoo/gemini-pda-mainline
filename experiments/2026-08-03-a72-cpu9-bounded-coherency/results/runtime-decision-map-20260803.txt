Hypothesis and attributable observation
---------------------------------------

The exact pair-v3 parent already proved twice that CPUs 8 and 9 can remain
online and execute three retained callbacks while bounded HPS down-pressure is
recorded. This child changes only the post-sample-2 observation: one CPU0
worker dispatches one synchronous call to CPUs 8 and 9 for a 1,024-round
shared-memory turn/sequence handshake with finite spin budgets and explicit
publish/consume barriers.

The only positive result is one complete pair-v4 terminal with all of these
values in the same line:

  result=pass sample=3 cpu8=8 cpu9=9 online8=1 online9=1
  hits8=3 hits9=3 hps_reported=1 hps_cpu=9 hps_error=-1
  hps_count greater than zero coh_reported=1 coh_rounds=1024
  coh_cpu8=8 coh_cpu9=9 coh_error8=0 coh_error9=0
  coh_seq8=1024 coh_seq9=1024

That terminal is decision-changing because the exact parent cannot emit
pair-v4 or any `coh_*` field. Marker text without the exact values is not a
pass.

Observation sequence
--------------------

1. In ordinary Gemian, run the reviewed installer against the exact candidate.
   It must resolve exactly one live-GPT logical `boot2`, prove it inactive and
   unmounted, record the predecessor checksum without a fresh backup, verify
   two full readbacks, remove temporary readbacks, and shut the device down.
2. Before physical selection, arm
   `scripts/collect-device-pstore --target gemini@192.168.1.50 --wait-for-cycle`
   as the primary changed-cycle observer. The optional USB/netcat collector is
   read-only and secondary.
3. Physically select `boot2` once. The expected visible behavior is the same
   bounded automatic watchdog restart as the exact terminal parent; screen
   color alone is not kernel evidence.
4. On immediate ordinary-Gemian recovery, require a changed boot ID, a
   watchdog-class recovery reason, CPUs 8 and 9 offline, an inactive/unmounted
   live-GPT `boot2`, and full boot2 checksum
   `eda1d5bb312aa937e41499ea8fd13a5f8ae95865399605fe7cf93ee61daaa23d`.
5. Search only the attributable changed-cycle pstore/console evidence for the
   complete pair-v4 terminal and fault exclusions.

Decision branches
-----------------

PASS
  Require the exact positive terminal above, automatic watchdog recovery,
  changed-cycle continuity, no panic, BUG, Internal error, Call trace, timeout,
  asynchronous SError, or unexpected fault, CPUs 8/9 offline after recovery,
  and unchanged boot2. Record a bounded-coherency runtime pass. One exact
  repeatability run is then earned; do not enable CPU_OFF or extend load yet.

PAIR-V4 FAULT
  Any `gemini-a72-pair-v4 result=fault` is a reject. Record every `hps_*` and
  `coh_*` value, recovery class, and exclusions. Do not repeat the unchanged
  candidate. Use the error/sequence boundary to choose the next source change.

INCOMPLETE OR WRONG TERMINAL
  Missing fields, `coh_reported` other than 1, rounds or sequences other than
  1024, wrong callback CPUs, any nonzero coherence error, or inherited HPS
  values other than reported=1/cpu=9/error=-1/count>0 is a reject. Do not infer
  coherency from online CPUs or earlier pair-v2 markers.

AUTOMATIC RESTART WITH NO PAIR-V4
  Classify as terminal-not-reached or evidence-loss using changed-cycle pstore,
  earlier pair-v2/HPS markers, watchdog reason, and console continuity. Do not
  repeat unchanged unless a new independent observation path can distinguish
  those classes.

NO AUTOMATIC RESTART OR LOST RECOVERY
  Treat as a safety failure. Recover through the known-good boot path, preserve
  available pstore, verify boot2 identity, and do not repeat unchanged.

UNEXPECTED NORMAL BOOT
  Verify the running kernel identity and boot2 full checksum. Treat the cycle
  as non-attributable unless the exact pair-v4 terminal and changed-cycle
  continuity are independently present.

Success boundary
----------------

One pass establishes only bounded shared-memory execution between retained
CPUs 8 and 9 under this diagnostic containment. A second exact pass establishes
repeatability. Neither result authorizes CPU_OFF, removes the HPS veto, proves
general cache coherency under load, or makes CPU8/CPU9 production-online.
