Kernel/DT/configuration hypothesis

serviceability path. The controller ledger independently records every I2C6
transfer shape while the provider attributes registration and observer reads,
then performs two fixed read-only passes over CONTROL_A 0x56, STATUS_B 0x51,
BUCKB_CONT 0x5e, VBUCKB_A 0xd9, and VBUCKB_B 0xda. The complete execution must
produce exactly the immutable 30-entry sequence in contract.json, with no
overflow and no register-data write.

Decision map
- Exact sequence, stable safe preflight, zero writes, and all serviceability /
  recovery gates pass: close Gate-6 blockers B3 and B4 only. Publish the
  sanitized record; B1 firmware-writer exclusion and B2 native two-byte write
  shape remain blocking. Do not perform a DA921x write or request CPU8.
- Exact ledger but unstable/locked/unsafe preflight: close B3 only; reject the
  proposed same-value transaction and localize the preflight mismatch.
- Safe preflight but sequence/phase mismatch, overflow, or failed transfer:
  keep B3 open and localize the first divergent entry; do not retry unchanged.
- Any write-only/register-data-write entry, PAGE_CON access, consumer/owner
  activity, CPU8/CPU9 request, or online A72: safety rejection and stop.
- No exact mainline identity before changed Gemian return: recover retained
  evidence once; screen state is not a kernel oracle.
- Fault or automatic reboot after exact identity but before the complete
  record: runtime failure; recover evidence and stop without causal overclaim.

Expected owner-visible sequence
1. The guarded installer writes only inactive logical boot2 and shuts Gemian
   down after exact full readback.
2. Codex arms the checksum-pinned USB/netcat collector and says it is ready.
3. The owner physically selects boot2 once.
4. On success, Codex captures the bounded record and requests native reboot;
   the device then returns to ordinary Gemian automatically.
