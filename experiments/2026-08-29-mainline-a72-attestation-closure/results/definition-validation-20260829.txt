Exact consumer findings
-----------------------

- Cache-type classification consumes target raw CTR, derived effective CTR,
  CLIDR, and current system CTR/strict mask.
- GICv5 legacy classification consumes the unmeasured ID_AA64PFR2 GCIE field,
  ICC_IDR0, ICC_SRE, and the GIC-SRE usability predicate.
- ICH_HCR_EL2_TDIR classification additionally consumes current hyp mode and
  ICH_VTR source/status/value.
- Spectre-v2 classification consumes the captured ID_AA64PFR0 CSV2 field and
  current SMCCC WA1 outcome.
- Spectre-v4 classification consumes the captured ID_AA64PFR1 SSBS field and
  current SMCCC WA2 outcome.
- BHB planning consumes the captured ID_AA64PFR0 CSV2 and ID_AA64MMFR1 ECBHB
  fields plus the unmeasured ID_AA64ISAR2 CLRBHB field.
- Current target policy comes from the mainline SMCCC conduit and mitigation
  command-line policy; Gemian policy is not admissible input.
- User HWCAP evaluation includes multiple unmeasured modern AArch64 registers.
  Missing prior-cycle fields must either conservatively remove the associated
  HWCAP or be enforced by a current-entry predicate; they cannot be zero-filled
  and called observed.
- The existing Linux 7.1.3 CPU-info path reads the modern AArch64 ID-register
  image and feature-conditionally reads AArch32, GMID, SMIDR, and MPAM state.
- Exact secondary C order is standard local-capability/HWCAP verification,
  CPU-info storage, GIC/timer notification, then online publication. The new
  expected-contract comparison belongs after CPU-info storage and before
  notification/online publication. Raw CTR and CLIDR need explicit entry reads
  because CPU-info stores effective CTR.
- ABI 7 still has no architecture commit, and the MT6797 production profile
  still has no system-verification or user-finalization callback.

Decision
--------

target contract. They cannot populate current-mainline observed fields or the
coarse complete ID_REGS validity bit. The next logical patch may add only a
field-valid expected-target schema and a fail-closed entry validator. It may
not add an active expectation, architecture commit, READY publication, CPU
request, CPU9 path, retry, CPU_OFF, or physical candidate.
