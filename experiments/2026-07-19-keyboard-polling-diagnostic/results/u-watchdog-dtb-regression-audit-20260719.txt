single-stage `mtk-wdt` registration, 31-second expiry, automatic return, and
cross-version console-ramoops path. Candidate P retained that exact no-IRQ
watchdog node and the loader-compatible simplefb node whose two retained clocks
had produced readable normal-landscape console output. Candidate U's keyboard
DT builder instead transformed the package DTB, not exact P. It therefore
reintroduced SPI137 and also dropped P's simplefb node and other candidate-only
LK handoff properties. U did not preserve either the visible-console or proven
watchdog DT foundation it was intended to extend.

The missing simplefb node is a concrete explanation for the lack of the
intended loader-framebuffer console if U reached Linux, but the visual result
still does not independently prove that U was selected or entered the kernel.
U's initramfs deliberately did not open `/dev/watchdog0`, so the watchdog DT
regression alone cannot explain its lack of automatic reset. A later candidate
must begin with exact P's DTB, permit only a precisely validated keyboard delta,
retain the no-IRQ watchdog and simplefb nodes byte-for-byte, open the proven
watchdog, send one ownership-handoff ping, retain the descriptor without
further pings, and recover pstore after the bounded automatic cycle.
