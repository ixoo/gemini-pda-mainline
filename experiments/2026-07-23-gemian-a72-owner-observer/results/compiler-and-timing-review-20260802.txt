Lock-context review
-------------------
- Ring writers use only the dedicated IRQ-safe spinlock and allocate nothing.
  Secondary-online and CPU-disable hooks therefore remain atomic-safe.
- DA9214 I2C work runs from the controller-side hotplug path, which can sleep;
  it uses the existing mutex. The observer record is appended after unlock.
- SPM mapping occurs before `__spm_lock`; fixed reads and RMW/readback are under
  that lock, and the observer record is appended after unlock.
- TOPRGU adds one readback under the existing register spinlock and appends its
  record after unlock.
- The MP2 DCM replacement uses one new local IRQ-safe spinlock for its two-write
  toggle sequence and readbacks, then appends after unlock. It introduces no
  nested owner/observer lock order.
- The protected-clock snapshot uses `spin_trylock_irqsave`; software-lock
  contention returns immediately. After success it holds IRQs disabled while
  attempting the hardware semaphore.
- The proc reader allocates before locking and formats after unlocking, but it
  copies the complete 212992-byte ring while holding the observer IRQ-safe
  spinlock.

Timing and observer-effect rejection
------------------------------------
The exact hook set invokes the broad fixed snapshot four times while bringing
an A72 online and four times while taking the last A72 offline. Each invocation
performs:

- three to six DA9214 I2C transactions, including page selection/restoration;
- six SPM MMIO reads under `__spm_lock`;
- twelve secure iDVFS reads plus a thirteenth sentinel read;
- one protected-clock hardware-semaphore attempt while IRQs are disabled; and
- one MP2 DCM read under the new DCM spinlock.

The protected-clock owner defines `SEMA_GET_TIMEOUT` as 2000 microseconds. The
snapshot duplicates that 200-iteration, 10-microsecond wait. Eight snapshots
therefore add a theoretical 16 ms of IRQ-disabled semaphore waiting over one
online/offline cycle, plus 104 secure calls and at least 24 I2C transactions.
Two online snapshots also sit around the vendor's prescribed 240-microsecond
SRAM-LDO intervals. These effects can change the transition being observed.

The owner-local mutation records themselves are bounded and retain useful
pre/request/readback evidence. The rejection is specifically the repeated
broad boundary snapshots, the waiting clock probe, and the oversized proc-copy
critical section.
