parser, then GNU ld rejected two common-symbol `yylloc` definitions. The lexer
and parser each contain one `YYLTYPE yylloc;`. GCC 10 and later default to
`-fno-common`; the selected kernel predates that change and expects the GCC 6
default `-fcommon` behavior. Target preparation had begun under pinned cross-
GCC 6.3, but the observer object had not yet compiled.

Decision
--------
Do not patch the shipped DTC source and do not alter target compilation. Pass
the tree-supported `HOST_EXTRACFLAGS=-fcommon` only to host-tool builds and
record that compatibility flag plus the host compiler identity in provenance.
Submit a fifth exact clean pushed commit. This result authorizes no packaging,
device deployment, or CPU8/CPU9 activity.
