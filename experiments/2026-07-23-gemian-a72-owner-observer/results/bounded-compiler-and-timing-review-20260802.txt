Owner-lock review
-----------------
The fifth patch adds no new lock and changes no owner-lock order. The previous
review remains valid:

- DA9214 records append after the owner mutex;
- SPM records append after `__spm_lock`;
- TOPRGU and MP2 DCM records append after their register spinlocks;
- ring writers allocate nothing under their dedicated IRQ-safe spinlock; and
- proc allocation occurs before its bounded ring copy.

The clock snapshot still uses `spin_trylock_irqsave`, but now holds IRQs disabled
only across fixed MMIO: one semaphore request/read, an optional 200 ns delay and
three register reads after acquisition, and one release/read. Software-lock or
hardware-semaphore contention returns immediately. There is no polling loop,
`udelay`, retry, warning, or fatal owner helper.

Timing bound
------------
The fixed broad-snapshot count is four over one complete online/offline cycle:

1. online pre, before transition mutation;
2. online post, after iDVFS enable;
3. offline pre, before iDVFS disable; and
4. offline final, after the kill/power-off path.

No broad snapshot remains in either vendor 240-microsecond SRAM-LDO interval or
inside `cpu_power_off_buck`. The rejected theoretical 16 ms IRQ-disabled wait
is therefore eliminated, not merely reduced.

A complete cycle still adds 52 fixed secure reads, 12 to 24 DA9214 I2C
transactions, 24 SPM reads, four immediate clock probes, and four DCM reads.
Those costs are outside the tight SRAM-LDO intervals and in the existing
controller-side hotplug context. They remain an observer effect and are
acceptable only for one bounded diagnostic capture with explicit identity,
event order, stop conditions, and recovery—not for production or an unbounded
stress test.
