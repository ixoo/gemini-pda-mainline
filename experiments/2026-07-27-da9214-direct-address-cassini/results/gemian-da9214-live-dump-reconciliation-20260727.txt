Exact active-binary PAGE_CON correction
---------------------------------------

The reconstructed active binary calls:

  da9214_config_interface(0x00, 0x01, 0x01, 7)
  da9214_config_interface(0x00, 0x02, 0x0f, 0)
  da9214_read_byte(0x05)

`da9214_config_interface` is a read-modify-write helper. The first call sets
PAGE_REVERT in bit 7. The second call's immediate value is `0x02`, but it
replaces only the low nibble and preserves bit 7; the final second PAGE_CON
write is therefore `0x82`. It is not a literal `0x02` wire write. This
supersedes the earlier wording in the active-binary audit.

Cassini RX-buffer ambiguity
---------------------------

Cassini's exact helper declares:

  uint8_t values[CASSINI_PASSES][CASSINI_REGISTER_COUNT] = {{0U}};

Each receive byte therefore starts as zero. Static inspection of the exact
Linux 7.1.3 paths shows that i2c-dev duplicates the userspace receive buffer
for `I2C_RDWR` and copies it back after a nonnegative transfer result; the
MT65xx controller maps that receive buffer for DMA from the device. Cassini's
six successful ioctl returns, six DMA starts, and six IRQs establish bounded
transaction completion, but do not independently prove that receive DMA
overwrote the initial byte.

Consequently:

  cassini_userspace_report=00,00,00-twice
  cassini_receive_prefill=00-for-each-byte
  receive_dma_overwrite=unproven
  received_zero_bytes=unproven
  live_gemian_tuple=d9,d0,c0
  da9214_identity_from_cassini=not-established
