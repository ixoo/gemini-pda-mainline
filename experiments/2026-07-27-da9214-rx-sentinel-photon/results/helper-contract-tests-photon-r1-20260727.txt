Interpretation
--------------

The tests exercise argument rejection, adapter uniqueness, transfer failure,
both complete sentinel extremes, persistent-line budgeting, result
classification, and exact-binary drift rejection without opening a real I2C
device. They do not establish Gemini hardware behavior; only the one-shot
full-readback-verified boot2 run can do that.

Revision note
-------------

R1 was never installed, booted, or invoked on the Gemini. Its exact tests and
embedded labels are preserved as historical artifact evidence, but
`overwritten` and `overwrite_mask` are not promoted to causal hardware facts.
Neutral r2 retains the exact `I2C_RDWR` request/message sequence and
successful-transfer control flow.
