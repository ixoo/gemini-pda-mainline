Interpretation
--------------

These tests prove the r2 helper's fixed transaction contract, objective
pre/post classifications, r1-equivalent `I2C_RDWR` request/message sequence,
and calibrated component identities without opening a real I2C device. The
durable harness is the installation gate. Earlier temporary wrapped-main tests
add supplemental evidence for the persistent-line budget and fail-closed error
boundaries, but their source was not retained and those reported results are
not treated as independently reproducible. No host-side test establishes
physical-bus or Gemini hardware behavior; only the guarded
full-readback-verified r2 boot and one-shot invocation can add hardware
evidence.

The only broader ShellCheck warning is historical Cassini foundation code:
seven SC2155 findings in `build-candidate-cassini.sh` lines 62--68. Photon r2
and its regenerated installer are ShellCheck-clean.
