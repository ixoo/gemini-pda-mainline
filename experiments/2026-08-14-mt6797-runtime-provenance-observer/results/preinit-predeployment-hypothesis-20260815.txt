Kernel/DT/configuration hypothesis
The changed kernel reaches its late-init-sync recovery companion after ramoops,
the MT6797 reset handler, the read-only provenance observer, and the vendor
watchdog kicker. It emits one attributable checkpoint to the registered pstore
console, schedules one 120-second delayed worker, emits one execution marker,
and calls emergency_restart() through the already registered MT6797 handler.
The corrected diagnostic ramdisk and appended DTB are byte-identical to the
previous attempt. RNDIS is a fast observation path, not the sole evidence path.

Unique attributable evidence
- the guarded full-partition write and readback bind boot2 to the exact padded
  candidate
- direct RNDIS/netcat, if available before reset, returns two mode-0444 observer
  snapshots with the exact candidate assertion and kernel release
- retained pstore contains the exact marker
  GEMINI_DVFSP_PROVENANCE_PREINIT_RECOVERY_20260815
- the checkpoint line must include recovery=armed and deadline_seconds=120
- the execution line must include reset=emergency-restart
- both marker lines must retain storage_access=none,
  dvfsp_hardware_write=none, and cpu8_cpu9_admission=closed
- ordinary Gemian recovery must have a changed boot ID after a confirmed
  disconnect/reconnect cycle

Owner-visible expectation
1. A verified boot2 installation ends with Gemian fully powered off.
2. Start boot2 once. The diagnostic ramdisk does not launch the stock Gemian
   splash, so its absence is expected and is not a failure signal.
3. RNDIS may appear briefly; no owner action is needed if it does not.
4. Near 120 seconds after late init, the candidate should automatically restart.
5. Allow ordinary Gemian additional time to boot. Do not manually restart during
   the first six-minute observation window unless the device is clearly unsafe.

Decision effect
- checkpoint + execution + changed recovery boot: kernel reached the recovery
  worker and the automatic restart is attributable; this localizes the prior
  failure beyond late init even if RNDIS remains absent
- checkpoint only: late init is localized, but delayed-worker execution is not
  established
- complete stable direct observer reads: accept vendor PPM/EEM lifecycle
  publication only
- no retained marker: retention/localization service failure; do not infer a
  kernel stop from display behavior alone
- any conflicting identity, write, owner/provider, fault, or CPU8/9 admission:
  reject and remain on ordinary Gemian

No outcome enables CPU8 or CPU9. No identical artifact is repeated unless a
new independent measurement is explicitly required.
