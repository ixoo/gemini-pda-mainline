MT6797 runtime provenance observer: pre-init recovery decision map
Date: 2026-08-15 America/New_York

success-runtime-publication
- exact retained checkpoint and execution markers
- changed recovery boot ID on ordinary Gemian
- exact direct candidate identity and kernel release
- two stable complete mode-0444 observer snapshots
- all lifecycle masks/counters complete and all nonclaims closed
Decision: accept vendor lifecycle publication and late-init recovery attribution
only. Do not enable CPU8/CPU9.

success-preinit-recovery
- exact retained checkpoint and execution markers in order
- exact recovery=armed, deadline_seconds=120, and reset=emergency-restart fields
- storage, DVFSP-write, and CPU-admission nonclaims unchanged
- confirmed disconnect, ordinary-Gemian reconnect, and changed boot ID
- direct RNDIS sample absent, unavailable, or not complete
Decision: establish recovery-worker reachability and attributable automatic
restart. Runtime EEM/PPM publication remains unobserved or inconclusive.

inconclusive
- exact retained checkpoint exists but the execution marker does not
Decision: localize the kernel through late init, but do not attribute the reset
or claim delayed-worker execution.

service-failure
- pstore console is absent, or neither exact retained marker exists
Decision: retained evidence failed. Display state alone does not localize the
kernel; do not repeat without a changed observation path.

rejected-attribution
- recovery cycle, candidate kernel, marker uniqueness/order, direct candidate
identity, or ABI identity is wrong or malformed
Decision: reject the sample as not attributable.

rejected-safety
- either marker changes storage_access=none, dvfsp_hardware_write=none, or
cpu8_cpu9_admission=closed; or the direct observer reports a fault, write,
owner/provider claim, or online CPU8/CPU9
Decision: reject the candidate and remain on ordinary Gemian.

All classifications keep cpu8_cpu9_admission=closed. A successful pre-init
recovery result improves failure localization; it does not itself establish a
DVFSP transition owner or advance CPU admission.
