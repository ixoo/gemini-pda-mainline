Manual source review
--------------------

- Patch 1 rejects CPU8 and CPU9 at public `cpu_down()` entry before RAM-console
  caller tracking, CPU-map locking, profiling, `_cpu_down()`, and every hotplug
  notifier. CPU0 through CPU7 are unchanged.
- Patch 2 identifies only exact HPS CPU bounds 8..9 and raises a zero target to
  one only while CPU8 is online. With online and target both one, the descending
  loop observes offline CPU9 and breaks before CPU8.

  invokes CPU8 synchronously at one and six seconds, requires exact callback
  CPU, CPU8 online, CPU9 offline, and hit count, and emits one terminal pass or
  fault marker.
- The hold path adds no load loop, CPU hotplug request, CPU_OFF, SRAM disable,
  control interface, watchdog change, or watchdog refresh.
- The exact one-way startup parent remains unchanged.
- Patches are experiment-only and have no synthetic DCO sign-off.
