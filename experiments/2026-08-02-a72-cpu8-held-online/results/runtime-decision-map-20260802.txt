Success class
-------------

All of these are required, in order and exactly once:

1. `gemini-a72-oneway-v1 result=cpu8-online-held stage=complete error=0`
2. `gemini-a72-hold-v1 result=sample sample=1 cpu=8 cpu8=1 cpu9=0`
3. `gemini-a72-hold-v1 result=pass sample=2 cpu=8 cpu8=1 cpu9=0`

There must be no held `result=fault`, no `result=down-veto`, no CPU9 online,
no cpuhvfs cluster-off notifier, and no panic before the pass marker. This
proves only bounded CPU8 execution/accounting through two synchronous IPIs. It
does not prove long-term stability, thermal safety, CPU_OFF, suspend, cpufreq,
or CPU9 readiness.

Failure and inconclusive classes
--------------------------------

Any `gemini-a72-hold-v1 result=down-veto`:
  The generic safety barrier dominated notifier entry, but an unexpected
  non-HPS caller requested CPU8/9 down. Reject the run even if IPI samples also
  pass; classify the caller and do not retry unchanged.

Any `gemini-a72-hold-v1 result=fault`:
  The synchronous IPI, callback CPU identity, CPU accounting, CPU9 exclusion,
  or rescheduling contract failed. Preserve the exact sample/error and do not
  retry unchanged.

Parent `result=rejected-prestate`, `result=rolled-back-preiso`,
`result=fault-retain-preiso`, or `result=fault-retain-postiso`:
  The already-proven startup did not reproduce. Preserve the last parent stage
  and return to parent-stage analysis.

cpuhvfs cluster-off notifier or panic before held pass:
  The early barriers failed to dominate the observed crash path. Reject the
  implementation; do not retry unchanged.

Missing marker, duplicate/conflicting marker, missing build identity, absent
changed-boot cycle, or pstore/live disagreement:
  Inconclusive. Return to known-good Gemian and improve attribution before any
  further boot.

Automatic restart with no exact retained marker:
  Recovery/serviceability only. It is not evidence about CPU8.

Deployment boundary
-------------------

The installer may write only the exact full candidate to the live-GPT-resolved
inactive, unmounted, writable 16 MiB logical `boot2`. It accepts only the exact
one-way predecessor (or skips an already matching candidate), requires Good
battery health and at least 65 percent capacity, synchronizes the write,
requires matching on-device and independent full-partition readbacks, removes
temporary staging, and shuts the device down. It records the predecessor hash
and creates no fresh backup. Primary boot, boot3, preloader, NVRAM, GPT, and
whole-device writes remain outside authorization.
