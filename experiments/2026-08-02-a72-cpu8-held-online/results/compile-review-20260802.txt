Binary control-flow review
--------------------------

- `cpu_down` begins by subtracting eight from the requested CPU, comparing the
  result with one, and branching CPU8/9 to a printk plus -EPERM return. The
  prior RAM-console recorder, locks and `_cpu_down` call occur only after the
  non-CPU8/9 fall-through.
- HPS first compares the exact cluster bounds with 8 and 9, tests CPU8 online
  when the target is zero, and conditionally raises that target to one before
  either power-up or power-down loop.
- The one-way completion retains its 48-byte frame, finishes the established
  CPU8 success path, initializes delayed work, converts 1000 ms, and queues the
  first sample.
- The 64-byte work function calls `smp_call_function_single` for CPU8, checks
  callback CPU8, CPU8 online and CPU9 offline, and converts 5000 ms before
  queueing its second and terminal sample.
- The 32-byte IPI callback obtains the executing CPU, stores it through the
  provided pointer, executes a full inner-shareable barrier and returns.
