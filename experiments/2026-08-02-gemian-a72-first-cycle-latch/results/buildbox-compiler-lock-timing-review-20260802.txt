  sleeps, prints, retries, and touches hardware zero times.
- Every sampling-state query releases the observer lock before acquiring an
  owner mutex or spinlock. DA9214, SPM, clock, DCM, and TOPRGU therefore add no
  nested observer-to-owner lock hold.
- Owner records still append only after DA9214, SPM, clock, DCM, or TOPRGU
  owner locks are released. No owner-to-observer nested lock hold is added.
- CPU9 overlap can freeze under the observer lock. A pure snapshot that passed

  hardware access if the state became terminal concurrently.

  after unlock. A completed natural pair is expected to stop far below that
