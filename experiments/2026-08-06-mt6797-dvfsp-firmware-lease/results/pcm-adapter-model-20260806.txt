The deterministic model checks the required admission ordering and rejects
premature callback registration, incomplete startup state, wrong CSPM/CSRAM
resource identity, unidentified image residency, stale state generation,
stale owner handles, and lease use after suspend/resume invalidation. It does
not select or copy a firmware image, map hardware, register a callback in the
kernel, or authorize a boot. It is design evidence only; a real protected
startup-state and PCM owner remains required before implementation can advance.
