the protected owner identity, and every paired transition hold to one opaque,
nonzero transition-owner handle. The contract is evidence-backed by the
historical vendor cpufreq transition lock, but this patch does not implement

registered, no secure or firmware operation is performed, and no device boot
or write was attempted. CPU8/CPU9 remain gated on an independently reviewed
