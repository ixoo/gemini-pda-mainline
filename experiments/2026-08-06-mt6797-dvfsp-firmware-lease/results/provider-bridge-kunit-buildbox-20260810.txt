Patch `0245` adds a KUnit fixture for the dormant calibrated-provider bridge.
It proves that incomplete callback tables are refused, complete identity/live/PPM/
calibration callback tables bind to the resource owner, owner callbacks can be
constructed, and provider exit invalidates before unbinding. The fixture never
invokes a callback, probes Device Tree, accesses MMIO, calls firmware or secure
monitor services, changes clocks or rails, registers a provider, or requests a
CPU. The successful Buildbox package validates compilation and linkage only; it
is not hardware support and does not authorize a device boot.
