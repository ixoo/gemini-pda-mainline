## Limits

The exact vendor ELF and the observer patch identify the same CSPM register
window and the same pause/status offsets. This is stronger than a generic
reset-state resemblance, but it is not proof that Candidate AN's one-way
receiver is the authoritative implementation of the vendor per-transfer
`SEMA_I2C_DRV` lease. Candidate AN disabled I2C6, did not write SW_PAUSE, did
not observe FW_DONE, and left I2C_APPM ungated. The result therefore cannot
authorize callback registration, a DA921x transaction, CPU8/CPU9 admission,
or a device boot.
