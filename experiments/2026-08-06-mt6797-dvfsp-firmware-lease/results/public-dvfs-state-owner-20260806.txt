The historical source now proves that a complete startup-state owner and
transition lock existed, including the exact fields required by the PCM
adapter. It does not make the vendor OPP tables portable: the tables depend on
efuse-selected variants, EEM/PTP mutation, PPM limits, live regulator state,
and vendor hardware owners. Mainline still lacks those owners, so this closes
the state-shape attribution but not implementation or hardware support.
