`mt_cpufreq_setvolt_registerCB()` at lines 1114-1120 and called by
`notify_cpu_volt_sampler()` at lines 1574-1586.  The observer itself only
converts the voltage and invokes the callback; it has no sleepable ownership
boundary and must not acquire the shared writer mutex recursively.
