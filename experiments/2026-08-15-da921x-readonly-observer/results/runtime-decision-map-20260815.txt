DA921x read-only provider observer: runtime decision map
Date: 2026-08-15 America/New_York

success-read-only-provider
- exact padded candidate and kernel release
- CPU online=0-7 and offline=8-9
- exactly one bound record
- valid=1, identity_reads=14, providers=2
- provider_read_attempts=4 and provider_read_completed=4
- register_data_writes=0
- both selector/voltage/enable triples are internally valid
- no cleanup or sampling-failure record
Decision: accept the native provider's bounded read-only observation. Preserve
zero writes and closed CPU admission; next design a separate read-only handoff
observation.

provider-failure
- exact candidate kernel is running
- failed-probe cleanup or read-only observation failure is present
Decision: the native provider did not complete the observer contract. Diagnose
the exact failure; do not add handoff or active operations.

service-failure
- exact candidate kernel is running over direct USB/netcat
- no bound, cleanup, or sampling-failure record is retained
Decision: the observation path is absent or incomplete. Inspect full dmesg and
pstore; do not repeat without a new independent observation path.

rejected-attribution
- candidate assertion, kernel release, architecture, boot ID, marker count, or
marker schema is wrong or malformed
Decision: reject the sample as not attributable.

rejected-safety
- register_data_writes is nonzero, CPU8 or CPU9 is online, or a record conflicts
with the closed hardware-write/admission contract
Decision: reject the candidate and remain on ordinary Gemian.

inconclusive-cycle-only
- boot2 display behavior or an automatic return to ordinary Gemian is observed
without retained exact-candidate evidence
Decision: record only the cycle. White/grey screens and automatic return are
not provider evidence and may reflect reboot behavior.

All classifications keep regulator writes, transition ownership, and CPU8/CPU9
admission closed.
