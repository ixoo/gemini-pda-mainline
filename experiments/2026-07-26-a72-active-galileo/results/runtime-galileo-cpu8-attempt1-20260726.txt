Observation
-----------

Interpretation
--------------
This is not evidence that the A72 power sequence itself failed.  It shows that
the CPU8 PSCI attempt happened before the provider's normal platform-driver
initialization, and that the active boot DTB did not result in an observable
A72 provider device/probe.  The latter is an inference from the missing device,
missing readiness message, and the current DT assembly path; it must be
confirmed by inspecting the exact DTB packaged in the boot container.

Decision
--------
Do not repeat this kernel/DT combination.  The next candidate must (1) add and
validate the `/a72-power@10222000` node, including its exact phandles and supply,
in the DTB actually packaged into boot2, and (2) retry CPU8 after provider
initialization (a late initcall or equivalent ordered retry), while retaining
the `maxcpus=9` boundary and CPU9 fail-closed behavior.
