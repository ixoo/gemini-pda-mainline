Acceptance boundary
-------------------

This fixes the exact artifact identity but does not authorize a write outside
the standing live-GPT-resolved inactive boot2 policy. Before deployment, the
installer must verify target identity, inactive/unmounted state, size, power,
predecessor checksum, full write/readback, and clean shutdown. The runtime map
must require the complete pair-v5 terminal and changed watchdog recovery.
