Acceptance boundary
-------------------

The exact child image is accepted only as input to deterministic Android-v0
container construction and independent offline reproduction. Before device
access, the raw and padded container checksums and embedded image identity must
be fixed, the pair-v5 runtime/recovery decision map must be executable, and the
inactive live-GPT-resolved boot2 write must retain its full readback and clean
shutdown gates. CPU_OFF and any changed power boundary remain out of scope.
