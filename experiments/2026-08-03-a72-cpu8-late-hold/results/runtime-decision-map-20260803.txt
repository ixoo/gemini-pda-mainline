Success class
-------------

All of these are required in order and without conflicts:

1. `gemini-a72-oneway-v1 result=cpu8-online-held stage=complete error=0`
2. `gemini-a72-hold-v2 result=sample sample=1 cpu=8 cpu8=1 cpu9=0 hits=1`
3. `gemini-a72-hold-v2 result=sample sample=2 cpu=8 cpu8=1 cpu9=0 hits=2`
4. `gemini-a72-hold-v2 result=pass sample=3 cpu=8 cpu8=1 cpu9=0 hits=3`

There must be no held-v1 terminal from the predecessor, held-v2 fault,
down-veto, CPU9 online, cpuhvfs cluster-off notifier, panic, Internal error, or
Call trace before the pass marker. This proves bounded CPU8 execution and
accounting through the late third synchronous IPI only. It does not prove
long-term stability, thermal safety, CPU_OFF, suspend, cpufreq, or CPU9
readiness.

Failure and inconclusive classes
--------------------------------

Any `gemini-a72-hold-v2 result=down-veto`:
  Reject the run even if the IPI samples pass. The generic barrier dominated,
  but an unexpected caller requested CPU8/9 down. Classify the caller; do not
  retry unchanged.

Any `gemini-a72-hold-v2 result=fault`:
  The synchronous IPI, callback CPU, CPU accounting, CPU9 exclusion, hit count,
  or scheduling contract failed. Preserve the sample and error; no unchanged
  retry.

Parent `result=rejected-prestate`, `result=rolled-back-preiso`,
`result=fault-retain-preiso`, or `result=fault-retain-postiso`:
  The proven startup did not reproduce. Preserve the last parent stage and
  return to parent-stage analysis.

cpuhvfs cluster-off notifier or panic before held-v2 pass:
  The unchanged early barriers failed to dominate the crash path. Reject the
  implementation; do not retry unchanged.

Missing sample 3 or terminal, duplicate/conflicting terminal, predecessor-v1
terminal without v2 evidence, missing build identity, absent changed cycle, or
pstore/live disagreement:
  Inconclusive. Return to known-good Gemian and improve attribution before a
  different candidate. Do not repeat this exact artifact unchanged.

Automatic restart with no exact retained marker:
  Recovery/serviceability only. It is not evidence about CPU8.

Deployment boundary
-------------------

The installer may write only the exact full candidate to the live-GPT-resolved
inactive, unmounted, writable 16 MiB logical `boot2`. It accepts only the exact
held-online predecessor (or skips an already matching candidate), requires
Good battery health and at least 65 percent capacity, synchronizes the write,
requires matching on-device and independent full-partition readbacks, removes
temporary staging, and shuts the device down. It records the predecessor hash
and creates no fresh backup. Primary boot, boot3, preloader, NVRAM, GPT, and
whole-device writes remain outside authorization.
