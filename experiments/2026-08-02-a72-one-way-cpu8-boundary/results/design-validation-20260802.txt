Validated boundaries
--------------------
- exact entry mismatch rejects without mutation;
- CPU9 rejects without mutation;
- SPM-reset, PWRAP, BUCKB-enable, and settle failures use the accepted
  pre-isolation rollback domain;
- an isolation write or readback failure preserves unknown isolation state;
- a PWRAP-deassert readback failure preserves unknown PWRAP state;
- SRAM request/readback failures never claim verified SRAM state;
- PSCI/result/secondary ambiguity never claims CPU8 offline or online;
- DCM failure after secondary completion preserves CPU8 online and unknown DCM;
- every post-isolation failure retains BUCKB and requires reset recovery;
- success requires secondary completion before DCM and ends CPU8-online-held;
- already-on and on-pending succeed only after affinity-ON plus secondary
  completion reconciliation.

Decision
--------
The abstract state machine is internally fail-closed, but this does not yet
authorize a kernel implementation. Next audit exact source insertion points,
owner-lock composition, watchdog and console-ramoops availability before the
natural request, maximum post-isolation time to the SRAM/PSCI boundary, and
terminal reset behavior. Only a positive review may generate experiment-only
patches on Buildbox.
