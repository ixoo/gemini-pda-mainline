Attributable forward sequence
-----------------------------

The retained checkpoints are exact and ordered:

1. `spm-reset`
2. `pll-ordering-read`
3. `pwrap-assert`
4. `buck-enable`
5. `isolation-write`
6. `pwrap-deassert`
7. `sram-readback`
8. `psci`
9. `dcm`

The SRAM service separately reported its 1.1 V setting successful. The exact
terminal record followed at kernel time 1.931991 seconds:

  gemini-a72-oneway-v1 result=cpu8-online-held stage=complete error=0 cpu9=forbidden cpu_off=forbidden

The first checkpoint was at 1.927944 seconds. The complete one-way transaction
therefore took about 4.047 ms; isolation clear to the success marker took about
1.906 ms. Immediately afterward the vendor power logs reported cluster 2 on at
845 MHz and HPS ended the up action with one big CPU accounted online.

Why this proves CPU8 online
---------------------------

The checksum-pinned source and machine-code review prove that this terminal
marker is reachable only after all of the following:

- the exact pre-state and watchdog takeover pass;
- BUCKB enable and settle, external-isolation clear, and PWRAP deassert pass;
- the SRAM-LDO secure request has an independent stable readback;
- standard PSCI CPU_ON returns success;
- generic ARM64 secondary completion occurs;
- Linux reports CPU8 online while CPU9 is absent; and
- MP2 DCM enable passes its exact final readback.

The marker occurred exactly once. It is therefore direct attributable evidence
that CPU8 came online for this candidate, not an inference from screen color or
the subsequent restart.

Post-success failure
--------------------

About 1.173 seconds after the success marker, the HPS thread entered the generic
CPU-down path. Before the platform CPU-disable callback could reject CPU8, a
CPU hotplug notifier called `cpuhvfs_notify_cluster_off` and took a translation
fault. The retained call chain is:

  cpuhvfs_notify_cluster_off -> _mt_cpufreq_cpu_CB -> notifier_call_chain ->
  _cpu_down -> cpu_down -> hps_algo_do_cluster_action -> hps_algo_main

The panic path reported failure to stop secondary CPUs and invoked the native
restart at about 4.679 seconds. Returned Gemian reports `kpanic` and
`wdt_by_pass_pwk`. Thus the observed restart was an attributable post-success
kernel panic, earlier than the independent watchdog deadline; it was not a
failure of CPU8 startup.

Decision
--------

Roadmap Gate 7 has passed its first online checkpoint but not stability,
bounded accounting/coherency, repeatability, or safe hotplug policy. Do not
repeat this unchanged candidate. The next smallest decision-changing change
must prevent HPS from initiating CPU8 down before generic CPU-hotplug notifiers
run, while retaining CPU8 online, CPU9 rejection, CPU_OFF rejection, the
watchdog fallback, and all existing startup gates. Review and compile that
change on Buildbox before another device write.
