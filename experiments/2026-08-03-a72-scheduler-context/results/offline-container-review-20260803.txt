Validation
----------

- Two independent ignored output roots each performed two raw assemblies and
  two independent padding constructions; all raw and padded outputs are
  byte-identical.
- Both roots pass the independent candidate validator with pinned assembler,
  pair-v6 parent assembler, builder, source builder, compile commit, patchsets,
  kernel, active-boot, ramdisk, raw, and padded identities.
- The raw container is 14,813,184 bytes and has Android-v0 magic, unchanged
  addresses, 2,048-byte pages, unchanged command line, and no second stage or
  separate DT extent.
- The embedded kernel is exactly 8,455,991 bytes and matches the final
  validated Buildbox child image.
- The retained 6,354,621-byte ramdisk matches the known-good Gemian input.
- The legacy image ID, aligned extents, zero padding, exact 16,777,216-byte
  partition size, manifests, and provenance validate independently.
- The builder contains no device path, network address, transport, reboot,
  shutdown, or partition-writing action. Runtime remains untested.

Acceptance boundary
-------------------

This fixes the exact artifact identity but does not itself authorize a write
outside the standing live-GPT-resolved inactive boot2 policy. Before deployment,
the installer must verify target identity, inactive/unmounted state, size,
power, predecessor checksum, full write/readback, and clean shutdown. The
runtime map must require adjacent complete pair-v6 and pair-v7 success terminals
plus changed watchdog recovery.
