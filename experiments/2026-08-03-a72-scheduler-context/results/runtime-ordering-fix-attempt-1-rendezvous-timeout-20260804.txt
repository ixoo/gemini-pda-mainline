Observation versus inference
----------------------------

Observed: `parent_pass=1` proves the corrected ordering evaluated the complete
published pair-v6 predicate before starting scheduler work. Both kernel threads
were created, entered ordinary task context, and ran on their exact bound CPUs.
CPU9 completed all 262,144 iterations with the exact expected hash. CPU8
started and ended on CPU8 but completed no workload iteration, retained its
initial hash, and returned `-ETIMEDOUT`. Both threads incremented the ready and
finished counters. Both parent completion waits expired against their shared
deadline. A zero `wake_up_process()` return is not treated as absence of task
execution because the task-context, CPU, ready, finished, and CPU9 completion
fields independently prove that both tasks ran.

Source-established failing phase: CPU8 can assign `-ETIMEDOUT` only while
spinning for `sc_ready == 2`. Therefore CPU8 entered first, reported ready, and
exhausted the bounded spin before CPU9 reported ready. CPU9 subsequently saw
the final ready count and completed normally. The evidence does not establish
why CPU9 was delayed relative to the spin budget; treating this as a CPU8
dispatch failure would contradict CPU8's exact task-context and CPU fields.

Next action
-----------

Do not repeat this image. Replace the asymmetric busy-spin rendezvous with a
bounded scheduler-friendly start protocol that cannot consume CPU8's budget
while waiting for CPU9 to be dispatched. Preserve exact task binding,

watchdog recovery, and serviceability boundaries. Generate and mutation-test
