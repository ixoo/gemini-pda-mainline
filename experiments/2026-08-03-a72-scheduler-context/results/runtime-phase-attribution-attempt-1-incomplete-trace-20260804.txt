Classification
--------------

Changed-cycle pstore and watchdog-class Gemian recovery make the restart
attributable. They do not create a continuous causal bridge from the unmatched
marker to reset. The fixed `FIRST UNMATCHED PHASE BOUNDARY` branch requires
continuous fatal/reset evidence after the marker, which is absent. The fixed
`ATTRIBUTABLE RESTART WITH INCOMPLETE TRACE` branch exactly covers a trace
continuous from `create8-before` that ends on a valid prefix without a
continuous fatal tail or matching after record.

Therefore record only:

- last completed retained phase: release;
- last retained unmatched marker: `done8-wait-before`;
- retained task phases: none; and
- exact artifact: rejected unchanged.

Do not claim that CPU8 failed, that the done8 wait caused or remained active at
reset, that either readiness wait timed out, or that the preceding candidate's
NULL dereference recurred. Full-size objects, clean final newlines, and matching
checksums establish faithful copying, not semantic completeness through reset.
