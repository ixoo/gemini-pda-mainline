Prepared finite child
---------------------

The new source-only contract preserves both historical patches byte-for-byte
and prepares one future logical `0003` that changes only
`arch/arm64/kernel/psci.c`:

- exactly two initial `wake_up_process()` calls become exactly two
  `kthread_unpark()` calls;
- each void call is followed by parent publication of
  `unpark_issued = 1`; no return value is invented;
- `wake_result`, `sc_wake8`, and `sc_wake9` become `unpark_issued`,
  `sc_unpark8`, and `sc_unpark9`;
- PASS requires both issuance fields to equal one; and
- the four parent wake phase names become `unpark8`/`unpark9` before/after
  names while the other 27 marker strings remain unchanged.

Creation, binding, ready/start/done completions, shared deadlines and release,
deterministic workload and hashes, placement checks, cleanup, pair-v6 gate,
HPS veto, CPU_OFF prohibition, power, and watchdog boundaries are outside the
finite replacement set.
