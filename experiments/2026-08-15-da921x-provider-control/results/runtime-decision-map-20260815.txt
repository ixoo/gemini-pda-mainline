DA921x provider-only matched control: runtime decision map
Date: 2026-08-15 America/New_York

success-provider-only-control
- exact padded candidate and kernel release
- CPU online=0-7 and offline=8-9
- exactly one DA9214 provider-is-read-only identity record
- exact I2C driver binding 1-0068
- exact read-only regulator pair BUCK0 and BUCK1
- no provider failure or observer marker
Decision: accept current-base serviceability without the observer and localize
the repeated failure to the observer-enabled delta.

provider-control-failure
- exact candidate kernel is running over direct USB/netcat
- identity record, binding, or regulator pair is absent/non-unique, or a
  provider failure is present
Decision: diagnose the exact provider-only runtime boundary; do not add an
observer, transition owner, setter, or CPU admission.

rejected-control
- any da921x-observer-v1 record is present
Decision: reject the sample because it is not the frozen provider-only control.

rejected-attribution
- candidate assertion, kernel release, architecture, boot ID, counter, or
  record schema is wrong or malformed
Decision: reject the sample as not attributable.

rejected-safety
- CPU8 or CPU9 is online or another closed safety assertion differs
Decision: reject the candidate and remain on ordinary Gemian.

pretransport-control-failure
- boot2 cycles to Gemian without exact USB/netcat or retained pstore evidence
Decision: the observer is not distinguished. Move localization to the current
base/container boundary against the last retained-pstore baseline. Do not
repeat this exact control without a new independent observation path.

Screen color and automatic return are not kernel or provider evidence. All
classifications keep hardware writes, transition ownership, and CPU8/CPU9
admission closed.
