Parser contract
---------------

- Requires the existing collector's confirmed stable disconnect, reconnect,
  changed boot ID, aarch64 recovery, exact recovery-kernel release, and
  pre/post-archive boot-ID binding.
- Reads only exact `pmsg-ramoops-0`; surrounding Android pmsg bytes are
  untrusted and may be arbitrary binary.
- Recognizes only the three source-pinned newline-terminated record forms and
  the mutually exclusive PASS/FAULT terminal.
- Accepts only prefixes of the exact sequence: none; entry; entry plus
  pre-scheduler; and entry plus pre-scheduler plus one terminal.
- The PASS terminal remains `pre-capsule-pass-await-capsules`; it is not
  promoted to a target-register result without independently valid capsules.
- Missing pmsg after a confirmed cycle is evidence loss or a pre-entry path,
  not a CPU result. Screen state is always non-classifying.
