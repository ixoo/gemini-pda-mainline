not a screen-color or apparent-reboot inference.

Repeatability classification
----------------------------

Attempts 1 and 2 used the identical full boot2 image. Each retained exactly one
self-contained pair-v3 pass after three reconciled callbacks on both CPU8 and
CPU9. Each published a complete HPS record for CPU9 with error `-EPERM` and a
positive matching-request count (91 then 89). Both excluded every predeclared
pair/startup fault, generic down-veto leak, panic, BUG, Internal error, and Call
trace; both recovered to Gemian through watchdog reason 4 with CPUs 8/9 offline
and the boot2 checksum unchanged.

The exact retained-execution/HPS-attribution repeatability gate therefore
passes. This proves only bounded callback execution and fail-closed CPU-down
pressure across two exact cycles. It does not prove general coherency under
load, long-term reliability, CPU_OFF, DVFS/OPP, thermal behavior,
suspend/resume, or default-profile readiness. The next ordered work is a new,
separately predeclared bounded coherency/load experiment; no unchanged third
run is justified.
