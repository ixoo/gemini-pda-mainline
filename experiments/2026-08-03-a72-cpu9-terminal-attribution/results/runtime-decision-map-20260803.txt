Pre-boot statement
------------------

Hypothesis:
  The exact proven PSCI-only CPU9 startup, three synchronous CPU8/CPU9 pair
  callbacks, public CPU-down veto, timings, and watchdog recovery remain
  unchanged. HPS naturally requests CPU9 down. The sample-3 terminal snapshots
  the completed first matching HPS -EPERM publication and total matching
  request count, making both facts durable in the retained console tail.

Unique attributable evidence:
  Exactly one terminal matching
  `gemini-a72-pair-v3 result=pass sample=3 cpu8=8 cpu9=9 online8=1
  online9=1 hits8=3 hits9=3 hps_reported=1 hps_cpu=9 hps_error=-1
  hps_count=N`, where N is a positive decimal integer. The pair-v3 terminal
  exists only in this candidate. Build identity and a changed watchdog cycle
  are also required. Screen color or an apparent reboot is not attribution.

Observation paths:
  1. Arm the optional read-only USB/netcat collector before manual boot2
     selection. USB may not enumerate within the fixed watchdog window.
  2. Arm `scripts/collect-device-pstore --target gemini@192.168.1.50
     --wait-for-cycle` before selection. Retain all records; delete none.
  3. On returned Gemian, prove changed boot ID, exact 3.18.41+ recovery kernel,
     watchdog-class reason, CPU8/9 offline, unchanged boot2 checksum, and an
     inactive/unmounted live-GPT-resolved boot2.

Success class
-------------

All of these are required in one attributable changed cycle:

1. Exact candidate build identity or exact pair-v3 candidate terminal.
2. Exactly one pair-v3 pass terminal with sample=3, cpu8=8, cpu9=9,
   online8=1, online9=1, hits8=3, hits9=3, hps_reported=1, hps_cpu=9,
   hps_error=-1, and hps_count greater than zero.
3. Changed-cycle watchdog recovery to known-good Gemian with CPU8/9 offline and
   the exact boot2 full-partition checksum unchanged.

If inherited pair-v2 samples 1 or 2 are retained, each must be unique, ordered,
show CPUs 8 and 9 online, and carry matching cumulative hit counts. Their
absence from the fixed-size tail does not invalidate the self-contained pair-v3
terminal.

There must be no pair-v2 fault, CPU9 rejected/fault state, parent startup fault,
generic `result=down-veto`, panic, Internal error, BUG, or Call trace before the
pass terminal. This proves bounded retained CPU8/CPU9 execution and HPS
attribution through the third pair only. It does not prove long-term stability,
load coherency, thermal safety, CPU_OFF, suspend, or cpufreq/OPP.

Failure and inconclusive classes
--------------------------------

Any `gemini-a72-pair-v2 result=fault`:
  Reject the run. Preserve sample, CPU identities, online bits, hit counts, and
  both errors; no unchanged retry.

CPU9 `result=rejected-prestate`, `result=fault-retain-psci`, or
`result=fault-retain-secondary`:
  The proven CPU9 startup failed to reproduce. Preserve the powered cluster
  until watchdog recovery and reject the run; no unchanged retry.

pair-v3 with hps_reported=0:
  No complete first HPS publication was visible by sample 3. Reject the
  attribution hypothesis; no unchanged retry.

pair-v3 with hps_reported=-1:
  The snapshot raced an incomplete publication. Reject the synchronization
  contract; no unchanged retry.

pair-v3 with hps_cpu other than 9, hps_error other than -1, hps_count=0, or
non-positive/non-decimal count:
  The expected HPS CPU9 -EPERM boundary was not proved. Reject; no unchanged
  retry.

Any generic `result=down-veto`, conflicting pair-v3 terminal, panic, Internal
error, BUG, Call trace, lost recovery, changed boot2 checksum, or unexpected
serviceability regression:
  Reject and return to the known-good path. Do not repeat unchanged.

Missing exact pair-v3 pass, missing build identity, absent changed cycle, or
pstore/live disagreement:
  Inconclusive. Improve attribution with a changed artifact; do not repeat this
  exact candidate unchanged.

Automatic restart with no exact retained marker:
  Recovery/serviceability only. It is not CPU9 attribution evidence.

Pass follow-up
--------------

One exact pass advances the CPU9 retained-execution boundary but is not a
reliability claim. Repeat this exact accepted candidate once with a new changed
cycle. Two exact passes then unblock a separate bounded coherency/load design;
they do not authorize CPU_OFF, DVFS, thermal, suspend, or default-profile
enablement.

Deployment boundary
-------------------

The installer may write only the exact full candidate to the live-GPT-resolved
inactive, unmounted, writable 16 MiB logical boot2. It accepts only the exact
retention-window predecessor or skips an already matching candidate, requires
Good battery health and at least 65 percent capacity, synchronizes the write,
requires matching on-device and independent full-partition readbacks, removes
temporary staging, and shuts the device down. It records the predecessor hash
and creates no fresh backup. Primary boot, boot3, preloader, NVRAM, GPT, and
whole-device writes remain outside authorization.
