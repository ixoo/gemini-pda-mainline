Pre-boot statement
------------------

Hypothesis:
  The exact proven PSCI-only CPU9 startup will remain unchanged. HPS will
  naturally request CPU9 down under low load; public cpu_down will reject every
  request at entry. The first HPS-observed -EPERM will produce one bounded
  attributable record, and later identical HPS warnings will remain silent.
  CPU8 and CPU9 will each complete synchronous pair callbacks about one, three,
  and five seconds after CPU9 completion, leaving roughly three seconds before
  the inherited fixed watchdog recovery.

Unique attributable evidence:
  One exact `gemini-a72-retain-v1 result=hps-down-held-first cpu=9 error=-1`
  record plus the unique `gemini-a72-pair-v2 result=pass sample=3` terminal
  with both CPUs online and both hit counts equal to three. The `v2` terminal
  exists only in this candidate. Build identity and a changed watchdog cycle
  are also required. Screen color or an apparent reboot is not attribution.

Success class
-------------

All of these are required in one attributable changed cycle:

1. Exact candidate build identity or exact `pair-v2` candidate marker.
2. Exactly one
   `gemini-a72-retain-v1 result=hps-down-held-first cpu=9 error=-1`.
3. Exactly one
   `gemini-a72-pair-v2 result=pass sample=3 cpu8=8 cpu9=9 online8=1 online9=1 hits8=3 hits9=3`.
4. Changed-cycle watchdog recovery to known-good Gemian with the exact boot2
   full-partition checksum unchanged.

If pair samples 1 or 2 are retained, each must be unique, ordered, show CPUs 8
and 9 online, and have matching cumulative hit counts. Their absence from the
fixed-size retained tail does not invalidate the exact sample-3 terminal,
which can be emitted only after all three synchronous rounds reconcile.

There must be no pair-v2 fault, CPU9 rejected/fault state, parent startup fault,
generic `result=down-veto`, panic, Internal error, or Call trace before the pass
terminal. This proves bounded retained CPU8/CPU9 execution and the fail-closed
public-down barrier through the third pair only. It does not prove long-term
stability, load coherency, thermal safety, CPU_OFF, suspend, or cpufreq/OPP.

CPUHVFS `cluster1 off` text is counted but is not by itself a failure or proof
of powered-off hardware. A later exact pair terminal directly proves both CPUs
online and executing callbacks. A CPUHVFS message followed by a pair fault,
panic, or missing attributable terminal remains failure or inconclusive under
the classes below.

Failure and inconclusive classes
--------------------------------

Any `gemini-a72-pair-v2 result=fault`:
  Reject the run. Preserve sample, CPU identities, online bits, hit counts, and
  both errors; no unchanged retry.

CPU9 `result=rejected-prestate`, `result=fault-retain-psci`, or
`result=fault-retain-secondary`:
  The exact proven CPU9 startup failed to reproduce. Preserve the powered
  cluster until watchdog recovery and reject the run; no unchanged retry.

Any generic `result=down-veto`, more than one `hps-down-held-first` record,
an HPS first record for CPU8, or an error other than `-1`:
  The bounded caller/reporting contract failed. The public barrier may still
  have dominated, but this implementation is rejected; no unchanged retry.

Parent `result=rejected-prestate`, `result=rolled-back-preiso`,
`result=fault-retain-preiso`, or `result=fault-retain-postiso`:
  The repeatable CPU8 foundation failed. Preserve the last exact stage and
  return to that boundary.

Panic, Internal error, Call trace, lost recovery, changed boot2 checksum, or
unexpected serviceability regression:
  Reject and return to the known-good path. Do not repeat unchanged.

Missing exact pair-v2 pass, missing/duplicate HPS first record, missing build
identity, absent changed cycle, conflicting terminal, or pstore/live
disagreement:
  Inconclusive. Improve attribution with a changed artifact; do not repeat this exact candidate unchanged.

Automatic restart with no exact retained marker:
  Recovery/serviceability only. It is not CPU9 retention evidence.

Deployment boundary
-------------------

The installer may write only the exact full candidate to the live-GPT-resolved
inactive, unmounted, writable 16 MiB logical `boot2`. It accepts only the exact
CPU9-parent predecessor or skips an already matching candidate, requires Good
battery health and at least 65 percent capacity, synchronizes the write,
requires matching on-device and independent full-partition readbacks, removes
temporary staging, and shuts the device down. It records the predecessor hash
and creates no fresh backup. Primary boot, boot3, preloader, NVRAM, GPT, and
whole-device writes remain outside authorization.
