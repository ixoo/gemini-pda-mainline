# Pruning pass, 2026-10-24: the measurement surface after the equivalence boot

Owner direction: once the tree is verified, take inventory of
measurement-only profiles, fragment lines and options and retire the ones
whose questions are answered, in one reviewable commit. This record is
that list. Retired files stay in the git history; no experiment record
is changed except two links that pointed at retired files.

## Kept, and why

- `mt6797-a53-wifi-lifecycle` (series `series-a53-wifi-lifecycle`,
  fragment `gemini-a53-wifi-lifecycle.fragment`): the one Wi-Fi profile
  every current and queued boot uses (pass-rate, receive DMA, AP-loss,
  station-record rate inputs). Its build options, all selected through
  `CONFIG_MT6797_MAC80211` or set by the fragment:
  `HIF_CORE`, `MAC80211`, `HIF_TX_STATUS_PROBE`, `HIF_TC4_RECONCILE`, `PASSIVE_SCAN`, `STATION_JOIN`, `NATIVE_DELIVERY_DIAG`, `CCMP_STABLE_EPOCH`, `CCMP_60S_WINDOW`, `CCMP_INITIAL_GTK`, `CCMP_CHANNEL_RELEASE`, `CCMP_CONNECTED_LIFETIME`, `CCMP_TX_PID_REUSE`, `CCMP_TX_PUMP`, `HIF_DMA_TX`, `CCMP_TX_DEPTH`.
- Every non-Wi-Fi profile (19 profiles remain in the manifest):
  board, service, Bluetooth and STP compile profiles are outside this
  pass.
- Proposals `0262` (TX-completion rate record, option
  `CONFIG_MT6797_TX_DONE_RATE_RECORD`, off by default) and the receive
  direction option `CONFIG_MT6797_HIF_DMA_RX` stay available: both are
  needed by queued boots.

These Kconfig symbols and the code under them are compiled into no
remaining profile. Removing them is a driver patch (one proposal, strict
checkpatch, compile-tested in the development VM, built once on Buildbox)
and is queued as its own change so that this metadata pass stays
reviewable on its own.
