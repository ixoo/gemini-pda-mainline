Success class
-------------

All of these are required in order and without conflicts:

1. `gemini-a72-oneway-v1 result=cpu8-online-held stage=complete error=0`
2. `gemini-a72-cpu9-v1 result=cpu9-online-held stage=complete error=0 cpu8=1 cpu9=1 cluster=1`
3. `gemini-a72-pair-v1 result=sample sample=1 cpu8=8 cpu9=9 online8=1 online9=1 hits8=1 hits9=1`
4. `gemini-a72-pair-v1 result=sample sample=2 cpu8=8 cpu9=9 online8=1 online9=1 hits8=2 hits9=2`
5. `gemini-a72-pair-v1 result=pass sample=3 cpu8=8 cpu9=9 online8=1 online9=1 hits8=3 hits9=3`

There must be no CPU9 fault/rejection, pair fault, down-veto, held-v1/v2 pass,
cpuhvfs cluster-off notifier, panic, Internal error, or Call trace before the
pair pass marker. This proves bounded CPU8/CPU9 execution, online accounting,
and shared-cluster reuse through the third synchronous pair only. It does not
prove long-term stability, load coherency, thermal safety, CPU_OFF, suspend, or
cpufreq/OPP safety.

Failure and inconclusive classes
--------------------------------

Any `gemini-a72-pair-v1 result=fault`:
  Reject the run. Preserve sample, CPU identities, online bits, hit counts, and
  both errors; no unchanged retry.

CPU9 `result=rejected-prestate`, `result=fault-retain-psci`, or
`result=fault-retain-secondary`:
  The CPU8/cluster prestate, standard PSCI call, or generic CPU9 completion
  failed. Retain the powered cluster until watchdog recovery, preserve the
  exact stage/error, and return to that boundary. No unchanged retry.

Any inherited `result=down-veto`:
  Reject the run even if pair samples pass. The barrier dominated, but an
  unexpected caller requested CPU8/9 down. Classify the caller; do not retry
  unchanged.

Parent `result=rejected-prestate`, `result=rolled-back-preiso`,
`result=fault-retain-preiso`, or `result=fault-retain-postiso`:
  The repeatable CPU8 startup did not reproduce. Preserve the last parent
  stage and return to parent-stage analysis.

cpuhvfs cluster-off notifier or panic before pair pass:
  The inherited barriers failed to dominate the crash path. Reject the
  implementation; do not retry unchanged.

Missing pair sample or terminal, duplicate/conflicting terminal, held-v1/v2
pass without pair evidence, missing build identity, absent changed cycle, or
pstore/live disagreement:
  Inconclusive. Return to known-good Gemian and improve attribution before a
  different candidate. Do not repeat this exact artifact unchanged.

Automatic restart with no exact retained marker:
  Recovery/serviceability only. It is not evidence about CPU9.

Deployment boundary
-------------------

The installer may write only the exact full candidate to the live-GPT-resolved
inactive, unmounted, writable 16 MiB logical `boot2`. It accepts only the exact
late-CPU8 predecessor (or skips an already matching candidate), requires Good
battery health and at least 65 percent capacity, synchronizes the write,
requires matching on-device and independent full-partition readbacks, removes
temporary staging, and shuts the device down. It records the predecessor hash
and creates no fresh backup. Primary boot, boot3, preloader, NVRAM, GPT, and
whole-device writes remain outside authorization.
