The public Gemian checkout independently reproduces the protected CPU-clock
ownership boundary and the absence of MT6797 ARMPLL/CPU-mux/CCI providers in
Linux 7.1.3. The MT6797 cpufreq source hash differs from the separately pinned
Planet reference, so this result is corroborating evidence only. It does not
authorize direct MMIO, a writable provider, a static OPP table, a PCM loader,
CPU8/CPU9 admission, or a device boot.
