Interpretation
--------------

Neither clock-backend checkpoint was recovered. The experiment therefore did
not establish execution of the clock driver's init function or its probe. It
also cannot distinguish failure to reach driver init from refusal inside the
shared exact-DT, reservation, prefix, mapping, or first-write path.

The candidate deliberately made no protected read, so it provides no result
about either protected transport. It also provides no CPU8/CPU9 admission or
hardware-support claim.

Do not repeat this artifact. Before another device boot, the next discriminator
must split the shared checkpoint preconditions through an independent durable
observation path or move to an earlier positively attributable stage. A new
candidate must remain storage-free, protected-call-free, and CPU/owner-closed.
