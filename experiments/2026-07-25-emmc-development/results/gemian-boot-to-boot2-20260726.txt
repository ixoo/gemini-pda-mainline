Operation: copy primary Gemian boot to logical boot2
Date: 2026-07-26
Device: named Gemini PDA under test
Transport: direct USB Ethernet development shell

Source and target
=================
- Live GPT source: `/dev/mmcblk0p22`, PARTNAME `boot`.
- Live GPT target: `/dev/mmcblk0p30`, PARTNAME `boot2`.
- Both partitions: 32768 sectors (16 MiB), writable.
- Target was unmounted and had no sysfs holders.
- No other partition was opened for writing.

Checksums
=========
- Primary Gemian source/readback image: `1fa78de9f8744a6818bcef2f6773737939f84364de982413910d4958d6d21513`.
- Full pre-write AW boot2 backup: `216daa467231d7a191b918584fa12fed6904429a020c1a2283fbf7ed82f74a0f`.
- Full post-write boot2 readback: `1fa78de9f8744a6818bcef2f6773737939f84364de982413910d4958d6d21513`.

Procedure and result
====================
The exact 16 MiB source was copied with `dd`, synchronized, and block-flushed.
The target was read back in full and matched the source checksum. The device
was not rebooted by this operation. The current AW shell therefore remains
running until the owner selects `boot2` on a subsequent boot.

Safety
======
The complete pre-write target image is retained locally at
`artifacts/device-partitions/gemian-boot2-20260726/boot2-before-gemian.img`,
with mode 0600 and its checksum recorded alongside it. No mount or filesystem
write was performed.
